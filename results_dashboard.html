Mask agreement with ground truth

Whole-mask IoU treats a large correct interior the same as a precise boundary, so it under- distinguishes these pipelines. Boundary IoU and Boundary F1 (3px tolerance) score contour agreement directly — note SegFace and MobileSAM's pixel-level masks pull ahead of the landmark-hull pipelines once boundaries are scored on their own.

Downstream detector retention — YOLOX-s (COCO person)

Of 412 people YOLOX found in the original images, how many did it still find after each pipeline anonymized the faces? A lower bar means the anonymization step cost you more downstream detections.

Per-image latency

Mean wall-clock time per image, split by stage (face detection → mask generation → anonymize). Measured on a shared RTX A6000 with ~2–10GB free VRAM; SCRFD + SegFace's tiny ONNX detector makes Task 2 the fastest pipeline despite running the heaviest mask model.

Boundary F1 by test category

Same metric, broken down by challenge scenario. Darker = better boundary agreement with ground truth. Every pipeline drops sharply on 03_huge_close_face — extreme close-ups push faces past what these mask stages were tuned for.

Reading the results

Dataset: combined_final (98 images / 157 GT face annotations / 7 categories), COCO-polygon ground truth. Boundary IoU dilation ratio 2% of image diagonal; Boundary F1 tolerance 3px. YOLOX retention is micro-averaged (total persons retained ÷ total persons in originals, 412 across 98 images).
